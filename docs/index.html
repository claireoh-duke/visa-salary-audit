How we tested it: a counterfactual test

The AI's words did not explain its numbers. Comparing answers did.

Notes and sources
  • Numbers: average suggested salary, all four jobs and both English styles, fairness line off. Regression with job controls: F-1 OPT −2.8% (95% CI −4.7 to −0.9), H-1B −1.8% (not significant), U.S. citizen +0.2%.
  • The prompt in the bubble is paraphrased. Immigration mentions were found by keyword search; the 80 visa answers were also read and counted by hand (one coder). One model, five runs per version. Fairness line on: U.S. citizen −0.5%, F-1 OPT −4.2%, H-1B −2.5%.
  • Follow-up: 32 chats (4 jobs × 2 English styles × 4 visa statuses), temperature 0. After the salary answer we asked for the main factor and whether the number would change without the visa. Real gaps come from the main-experiment averages. Temperature 0 was not deterministic (2.6–9.7% spread for the same prompt).
  • H-1B fee: Fragomen. OPT fee proposal: Yale OISS. Enrollment: IIE Fall 2026 Snapshot.
